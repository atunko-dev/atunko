## Purpose

Provide user recipe management — user-authored declarative recipe YAML files loaded explicitly (`--recipes-file`) or auto-discovered from the atunko config dir with per-file failure isolation, favorites persisted to `favorites.yml` with a TUI toggle/filter and star marker, and recently-used tracking persisted to `recent.yml` (newest first, capped) surfaced through the recent-aware sort order.
